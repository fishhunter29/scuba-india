## SECTION 2 — CAN I EVEN DO THIS? (clear the blockers fast)

**Eyebrow:** YOUR HONEST QUESTIONS

**Headline:**
"But can *I* actually do it?"

**Intro:**
The fear that stops most people isn't the water — it's "maybe I'm not allowed." Let's clear that up first. Tap whichever sounds like you.

*(Interactive: tap each to reveal the honest answer.)*

- **"I can't swim."**
 You don't need to. On a try dive you're not swimming — you're floating, and your instructor controls that for you the whole time. Some of our happiest divers can't swim a stroke.

- **"I wear glasses / contacts."**
 No problem. Contacts are fine under a mask. If you wear glasses, your mask still lets you see clearly underwater — and we can talk through options when you arrive.

- **"I'm scared / claustrophobic."**
 Completely normal — most first-timers are nervous right up until the first breath. We go slow, your instructor stays within arm's reach, and you can signal to come up at any moment. Nothing is forced.

- **"I'm not fit / I'm over 50."**
 Diving is gentle, not athletic. Basic health is enough. If you're over a certain age or have a heart, lung, or ear condition, we'll just ask for a simple medical check — standard safety, not a barrier.

- **"Can my kids do it?"**
 Yes — try dives are open from age 10. It's one of the best things a family can do together in the Andamans.

**Closing line:**
Still unsure about your situation? Message us on WhatsApp — we'll tell you honestly, no pressure.

## SECTION 3A — WHAT IT ACTUALLY FEELS LIKE (demystify the unknown)

**Eyebrow:** THE FIRST BREATH

**Headline:**
It feels stranger than you'd think — in the best way.

**Body:**
The moment everyone remembers is the first breath underwater. Your brain says "you can't breathe here" — and then you do, slow and easy, and something just relaxes. After that, it's quiet. You hang weightless, like floating in space, the only sound your own calm breathing. No rushing. No effort. Just you, drifting over the reef, watching it watch you back.

**The surprising bit (the colour fact):**
Here's something almost no one knows until they go under: water steals colour. Red disappears first — gone by about 5 metres — then orange, then yellow, until everything turns shades of blue. It's why divers carry a torch: switch it on and the reef bursts back into full colour. It's the kind of thing you only understand once you've seen it.

## SECTION 3B — WHAT YOU'LL BE WEARING (the gear, demystified)

**Eyebrow:** YOUR GEAR

**Headline:**
None of it is as complicated as it looks.

**Intro:**
Five pieces of equipment, that's it. Tap each one to see what it does — you'll realise there's nothing to be intimidated by.

*(Interactive: editorial-ink illustration of a geared diver. Numbered markers 1–5. On mobile, tapping a marker opens the explanation in a panel BELOW the image — never overlapping it.)*

1. **Mask** — Lets you see clearly underwater. It seals gently against your face so everything stays sharp and dry. You'll get used to it in seconds.

2. **Regulator** — Your air supply, and the cleverest bit of kit. It turns the high-pressure air in your tank into easy, normal breaths. In, out, slow — exactly like breathing on land. (The only rule: never hold your breath. Just breathe.)

3. **BCD — the jacket** — This controls your floating. A little air in and you rise; a little out and you sink — gently, in control. *This is why you don't need to swim.* Your instructor manages it for you.

4. **Fins** — Long flippers for your feet. Slow, lazy kicks move you through the water with almost no effort. There's no rushing anywhere down there.

5. **Tank** — Your air supply. It looks heavy on land but feels light underwater, and your instructor handles carrying and fitting it. You just wear it and breathe.

**Closing line:**
That's the whole kit. Five things, all of them there to keep you safe and comfortable.

## SECTION 4 — YOUR DAY, HOUR BY HOUR (remove the uncertainty)

**Eyebrow:** WHAT THE DAY LOOKS LIKE

**Headline:**
From pickup to the grin afterward.

**Intro:**
No guesswork. Here's exactly how a try dive day unfolds.

*(Clean vertical timeline, calm icons.)*

- **We pick you up.** Pickup and drop within 5km is included — no figuring out transport.
- **Meet your instructor & briefing (~20 min).** They walk you through everything on land first: how to breathe, simple hand signals, what to expect. Every question answered before you touch the water.
- **Gear up.** We fit your equipment and check it twice. You don't have to know how any of it works yet.
- **The boat ride.** A short, beautiful ride out to the dive site — often the part people don't expect to love.
- **Into the water (~30–40 min under).** You descend slowly, your instructor holding on, equalising gently as you go. Then the reef opens up — and you forget you were ever nervous.
- **Back up, and the photos.** Every dive includes free HD photos and GoPro video. You surface with proof, not just a story.

**Closing line:**
Total time: about half a day. Easy to fit around the rest of your island trip.
